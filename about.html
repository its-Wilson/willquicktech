Why we started this

Built on a refusal to settle for "good enough."

"I started Quick Tech Consulting out of a genuine passion for technology — and a frustration with how often infrastructure gets treated as an afterthought. Too many businesses are paying for performance they aren't actually getting: systems that are misconfigured, bottlenecked, or running well below what they're capable of. Leading cloud security work at AWS and hardening mission-critical systems — including satellite ground infrastructure, where downtime and breaches were never acceptable outcomes — taught me what a genuinely high bar for technology looks like. I wanted to bring that same standard to businesses that don't have the budget for their own in-house team: infrastructure that's properly configured, tuned to actually perform, and free of the bottlenecks that quietly cost a business time and money. Not good enough. Actually good — and actually running the way you're paying for it to."
QTC
Founder, Quick Tech Consulting
