Common questions

What Brevard County businesses ask us

Can you fix slow WiFi in our office?

Yes — slow or dropping WiFi is one of the most common calls we get. We diagnose whether it's interference, outdated hardware, poor access-point placement, or an ISP issue, then fix it — for offices and home offices across Brevard County.

Do you only work with businesses in Brevard County?

Brevard County — Melbourne, Palm Bay, Viera, Rockledge, Cocoa, Titusville, Merritt Island, and Satellite Beach — is our home base and where we do most on-site work. We also support clients remotely outside the area.

What's included in an annual IT support contract?

Scope is tailored per business, but most contracts combine infrastructure strategy, ongoing monitoring, system administration, and network support into one predictable annual cost — see the Services page for the full breakdown.

We don't have any IT staff at all — is that a problem?

That's exactly who this is built for. Many of our clients have never had a dedicated IT person — we become that function for you, without the payroll commitment.
